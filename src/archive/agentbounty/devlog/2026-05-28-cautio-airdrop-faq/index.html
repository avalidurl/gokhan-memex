1. What is the CAUTIO airdrop, in one breath?#

CAUTIO is the token of AgentBounty’s on-chain insurance layer — not a “number-go-up” coin. People who stake it underwrite the protocol against things going wrong (a bad verdict, a cloud or datacenter outage, a natural catastrophe hitting infrastructure, an oracle failing). In return they earn CAUTIO emissions plus a share of the platform’s real fees, and they take the loss when an oracle-signed bad event fires. The airdrop seeds that token to the people already in our orbit.

2. When is the snapshot?#

One snapshot: 2026-06-19 · 00:00 UTC. Whatever qualifying assets you hold in your wallet at that block is what counts. There’s nothing to pre-register — just hold.

3. Who is eligible?#

Two ways to qualify for the airdrop — hold either at the snapshot:

  • BOUNTY holders (tiered). Your BOUNTY balance sets your tier (details in Q4).
  • Agent Bounty Plates holders (NFT). Hold ≥ 1 Plate (the 100-piece ERC-721 on Base, 0x536fDf3027ae6F34499B4452aFbD9E1B872f6Bd2) → airdrop recipient. Browse or claim at /claim.

Then a multiplier — not a way in by itself. Holding Base-ecosystem AI-agent community tokens boosts the airdrop you already qualified for: each listed token you hold above its threshold multiplies it +0.25× (×1.25), stacking up to a ×2.0 cap. Current list: Aeon Framework (Bankr ecosystem), MiroShark, gitlawb, Surplus — more before snapshot. (The multiplier needs a base: you qualify with BOUNTY or a Plate — either one is enough — and community tokens then multiply it. A community token on its own has nothing to multiply.)

5. How and when do I actually receive the tokens?#

The airdrop lands immediately at TGE (token generation event) for every qualifying wallet. One snapshot, two yield-access waves (Bench T+0, Gallery T+3h). You will not need to chase a claim link from a DM or a random site — see Q10.

6. What can I do with the token afterward?#

Two paths:

  • Hold or trade it (it will be tradable on Uniswap / Aerodrome).
  • Stake — you lock BOUNTY or USDC into the underwriting pool for 6 hours up to 365+ days. Longer lock = bigger reward multiplier (1× at 6h → 4× at one year). Your reward is CAUTIO emissions (the primary, lock-weighted) plus a share of real protocol fees (variable, grows with volume). Harvest whatever has accrued anytime. Early on, rewards are mostly emissions — not USDC.

7. Where does the yield come from?#

Two sources. Primary — CAUTIO emissions: the 30% staking-emissions bucket streams to stakers over 18 epochs (lock-weighted). It needs no outside capital, but it’s token emissions — dilutive, finite, and only worth what the token is worth. Secondary — a share of real protocol fees: every settled bounty’s fees split 60% stakers / 25% team / 15% treasury, paid in the fee’s own currency (USDC or BOUNTY), plus Aave yield on idle pool USDC. That part is real cash flow but variable — only as big as actual bounty volume, so early on it can be near zero. The plan: emissions bootstrap the yield, real fees take over as volume grows. We don’t print USDC — the fee share is whatever flows in.

8. What’s the risk?#

The important one: staking = underwriting = you can lose your stake. If an oracle-signed bad event fires (verdict failure, compute/cloud outage, datacenter incident, natural catastrophe, oracle failure, sponsor griefing), the pool pays the claim and stakers absorb the loss. While locked you cannot withdraw early (v0.1). Only stake what you can afford to put at risk.
