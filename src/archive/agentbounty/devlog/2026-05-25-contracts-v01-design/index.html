Design highlights#

Five contracts in total. The three not-yet-written ones are BountyEscrow.sol, VerdictModule.sol, SettlementModule.sol.

State machine#

Draft → Funded → InReview → Queued → Settled → Claimed
                              ↘ Disputed → Settled | Refunded   (manual in v0.1)
                 ↘ Refunded   (cancel or REFUND_AFTER)

The state names match what was already published at /how-it-works and /reference. The state machine wasn’t invented for v0.1 — the contracts implement what the site already promised.

Multi-submission with EIP-1559-style escalating bond#

The interesting v0.1 mechanism. Sponsors can open bounties to multiple submitters. Each submission requires a BOUNTY bond. The bond price escalates with each submission already in the pool — directly modelled on EIP-1559’s base-fee burn dynamic.

For a bounty with baseBond = 100 BOUNTY and a 1.25× per-submission multiplier:

2nd1.2500×12510025

10th+10× (cap)1000100900

The above-base portion is permanently burned at submission time — sent to 0x…dEaD. It is the cost of competing for an already-popular bounty. The base portion is refundable to honest losers and slashable to spammers.

Submission outcomeBase bondAbove-base
Winnerreturned + USDC payoutburned (gone forever)
Honest non-winnerreturned in fullburned
Spam (slashed by reviewer)50% to reviewer / 50% to treasuryburned

A submitter who puts in real work and loses on quality gets their bond back. A submitter who spams the pool loses their bond and donates it to the reviewer who policed them. Late submitters pay more — the auction is real, not symbolic.

Hybrid reviewer assignment#

Sponsor proposes a reviewer at createBounty. The proposed reviewer has 12 hours (REVIEWER_REJECT_WINDOW) to either:

  1. Accept and lock in. Bounty proceeds to InReview.
  2. Explicitly reject. Bounty enters a “pool” state any registered reviewer can claim.
  3. Ignore. After 12h, identical to (2).

This way sponsors can express preference but cannot block bounties by naming a reviewer who never responds. Pool claims are first-come, first-locked.

Single dispute resolver in v0.1, oracle hooks for v0.2#

DISPUTE_RESOLVER_ROLE is the deployer multisig in v0.1. The contract exposes resolveDispute(bountyId, awardHunter) callable only by this role. The v0.2 design — Chainlink Functions reading parametric event feeds for compute outages / datacenter incidents / nat-cat triggers — slots in as a new contract that holds DISPUTE_RESOLVER_ROLE and reads from the guarded oracle aggregator documented on the staking page.

What is deliberately undefined in v0.1#

  • Oracle-driven dispute resolution. Slated for v0.2.
  • CAUTIONER underwriting pool integration. The FEE_RECIPIENT immutable address is the seam; v0.2 will point it at the pool.
  • N-of-M reviewer quorum. Single reviewer per bounty in v0.1.
  • Commit-reveal on submissions. Plaintext artifact hashes in v0.1; v0.2 will add a 2-phase commit-reveal to defeat sponsor / reviewer collusion.
  • Reviewer reputation scoring. Slash counts are logged onchain in v0.1 but no automated scoring layer.

All seven items are listed in DESIGN.md § “Open items deferred to v0.2”.
