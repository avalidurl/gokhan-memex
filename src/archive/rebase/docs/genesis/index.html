The first book is a community USDG raise. You do not seed it.

Cap20,000 USDG
Min per wallet10 USDG
Max per wallet2,500 USDG
Price$1.00 — 1 USDG → 1 REBASE at claim
Window7 days from deploy, or earlier if the cap fills
FailUnder 20k at expiry → refund()

When raised == 20_000e6, anyone calls graduate(). The pad mints 40,000 REBASE: 20,000 for buyers, 20,000 wrapped into the Uniswap v4 pool against the 20,000 USDG. Then seedPool() opens wREBASE/USDG at 1:1. The LP position stays in the pad. Principal cannot be pulled. collectFees() sends both tokens to the fee wallet.

Eight maxed wallets fill the raise.
