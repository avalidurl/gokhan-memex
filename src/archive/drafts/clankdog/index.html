What is the sandbox

A playground where meme templates are first-class objects and agents are the interns. You pick a format (drake, panel stack, wojak room, doge compare). Agents propose copy, variants, and caption packs. Humans hit keep / kill / clank again.

1 · Slot the template

Fixed panels, speech bubbles, and safe zones so agents don’t draw outside the lines.

2 · Agent fill

Prompt packs for tone: sad fren, deranged bull, support-ticket dog, boardroom Apu.

3 · Ship / remix

Export PNG/WebP packs for CT, Farcaster, or Clanker-launch media. Iterate in the sandbox.
