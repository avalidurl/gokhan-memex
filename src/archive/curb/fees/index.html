How fees work §1

LP / protocol layer. CURB launched on Bankr (Doppler) on Base. On-chain reward share is 95% creator (goekhan Bankr EVM claimer) / 5% protocol — verified via bankr fees --token. That layer does not yet multi-split to team/treasury/burn on-chain; claims land at the creator claimer first.

Distribution (creator-side). Claimed creator fees follow the same Numetal policy as numetal.xyz/fees: WETH side 80% team / 10% treasury / 10% burn; $CURB side 40% team / 30% treasury / 30% burn.

Hub swap fees. On CURB inference matches, payment is single-payee x402: the buyer pays gross ONCE to an on-chain 0xSplits split on Base; the split fans out seller wallet + hub fee (5.0%, min $0.001 USDC) on-chain. Hub never holds upstream LLM keys.

Team / treasury wallets are the same Numetal Labs addresses published on numetal.xyz/fees.
