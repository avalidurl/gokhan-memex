What we collect§1

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address you submit and, optionally, the wallet address and role you provide. Our server briefly records the requesting IP for abuse prevention and rate-limiting. The wishlist vote records an aggregate count only — no personal data.

Why§2

Solely to notify you about the ACCUMULATOR / $ACC launch and related updates, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party trackers on this site.

Where it lives§3

Submissions are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV under the site's account. Email delivery, when it happens, runs through the estate's newsletter system (feedhub / Resend) under a single, double-opt-in list across Numetal / Ishtar / CURB / the studio.

Your choices§4

Ask us to remove your record at any time — contact@gokhan.vc. Once the newsletter is live, every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.
