This Privacy Policy explains how **Gökhan Ventures** (“we”, “us”) handles information
in connection with the website at **gokhan.vc** (the “Services”). We collect as little
as possible.

## Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect personal information through gokhan.vc. The site serves static
content only. Our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical request data
(such as IP address and browser metadata) to deliver and secure the site. If you choose to email
us at **[contact@gokhan.vc](mailto:contact@gokhan.vc)**, we receive the contents of your message and your email
address solely to respond.

## How we use information

We use the limited information above only to operate, maintain, and secure the Services. Our
**lawful basis** is our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and delivering the
Services (GDPR Art 6(1)(f)). We do **not** sell or share personal information, and we
do not use it for advertising, targeted advertising, or profiling (including under the CCPA/CPRA).

## Service providers

We rely on third-party infrastructure to run the Services: **Cloudflare** (hosting, security,
delivery). The site also links out to external platforms (such as X, GitHub, LinkedIn, Farcaster,
Bluesky, Substack, Telegram, and Discord); if you follow those links, the destination platform processes
your data under its own policies.

## Cookies & tracking

gokhan.vc does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly
necessary cookies for security and to operate the site. See our
[Cookie Policy](/gokhan-vc/legal/cookies/) for details.

## Data retention

We retain technical/security logs only as long as needed for security and operation. Any email
you send us is retained only as long as needed to handle your enquiry.

## Your choices & rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to **access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port** your personal data, to **object** to processing, and to **lodge a complaint with your data-protection supervisory authority** (GDPR Art 77). To exercise any of these, contact [contact@gokhan.vc](mailto:contact@gokhan.vc).

## Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 16 (or the applicable digital-consent age in
your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest
version. Continued use of the Services after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated
policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [contact@gokhan.vc](mailto:contact@gokhan.vc). See also
our [Terms of Service](/gokhan-vc/legal/terms/).
