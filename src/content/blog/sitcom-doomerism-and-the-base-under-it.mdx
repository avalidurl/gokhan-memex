**Item #:** SCP-NYT

**Object Class:** Uncontained

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-NYT is not contained. All prior attempts at containment have failed. The entity self-propagates through print, digital, and institutional channels faster than any known amnestic or memetic countermeasure can suppress.

Personnel are advised that “isolating” SCP-NYT to a single server, newsroom, or legal jurisdiction produces no measurable reduction in effect. Temporary workarounds (paywalls, subscription models, or “corrections” appended after market impact) have been observed to increase rather than reduce spread.

The Foundation has reclassified SCP-NYT as Uncontained following the 2026 incident in which a single article, widely described by external observers as unsourced, coincided with the erasure of approximately $2 trillion in market capitalization. No further containment research is authorized; resources are to be redirected toward documenting downstream effects.

Description: SCP-NYT is a self-sustaining informational entity that manifests as a news organization. When it publishes certain claims, large quantities of previously recorded economic value cease to exist within consensus reality. The mechanism does not appear to require the claims to be accurate, named-sourced, or subsequently verified.

The entity demonstrates resistance to ordinary corrective measures. Attempts to “contain” it by demanding primary sources, legal liability, or editorial accountability result in further dissemination under the framing that such demands constitute attacks on free inquiry.

Current status remains Uncontained.
