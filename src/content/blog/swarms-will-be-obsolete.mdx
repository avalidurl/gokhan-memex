## The post it answers

**The Bitter Bitter Lesson**, from [artlu99's blyg](https://blyg.artlu.xyz/), canonical URL [https://blyg.artlu.xyz/t/0hzp5c1bppnjmdd6tcpg1vyff8/](https://blyg.artlu.xyz/t/0hzp5c1bppnjmdd6tcpg1vyff8/). v2, 13 November 2026.

> There is the well-known and oft-cited Bitter Lesson for AI researchers.
>
> But unless you are an AI researcher, and as of two years ago there were only maybe 2000 of you on earth, there is another Bitter Bitter Lesson.
>
> It's not just model improvement that makes special learning obsolete. It's tooling improvement, by severely autistic folks willing to ride the wave of The Bitter Lesson, that multiplies the Bitter Lesson for you and for me.
>
> If you were a hobbyist 12 months ago, maybe into better agentic memory systems or self-improving agentic workflows, it's 99% odds that everything you have worked on is now obsolete, or at best, absolutely sub-optimal compared to free tooling that you can access via Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, Command Code, Amp, Grok Bot, etc. If you had a decent but finicky OpenClaw or Hermes setup, and muscled it into a much better shape, you're still basically level with newcomers onboarding onto Muse or Instinct today.
>
> If you were a professional programmer 12 months ago, diligently keeping your skills sharp as a centaur 20x eng, then sub-agent swarms, software factories, and now swarm riding, you are still kinda level with engineer Grandpa given access to the right off-the-shelf tooling and a great one-line mental frame ("set up goals that the agent can verify"). All the harness memory wiring and eval loop scaffolding you did? It basically only kept your head above water for a bit, until more people could jump into the same pool.
>
> How do I know this? I sat out the whole software factory phase. And now I ride swarm nearly as good as the the best swarm rider. She just started maybe two months ago.
>
> Huh.

Their footnote: if you were among the 1000 or so near SF, you are now a centimillionaire to multi-billionaire. If you were among the 900 or so near Beijing or Shenzhen, you are now a decimillionaire. If you were among the 100 or so in Europe, you have probably moved already to SF or Asia.
