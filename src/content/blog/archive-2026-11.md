## Writing

Every essay from the Numetal, Gökhan Ventures, Ishtar and CURB blogs is in the [Journal](/journal/), tagged by product. The [tag pages](/archive/tags/) gather each product with its posts, drafts and APIs.

The shared estate newsletter will send a last issue. Unsubscribe links in past issues keep working.

## Services

Paid APIs, agent endpoints, MCP servers and waitlists will close. Their documentation is kept in the [API index](https://api.gokhanturhan.com/). Calls to retired endpoints will return a notice that points to the product page.

## What stays

Source repositories that were public stay public. Hugging Face models, Spaces and datasets stay where they are. Onchain contracts are unchanged. The email addresses keep working.
