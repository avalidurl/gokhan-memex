Abstract§0

An accumulator is two things at once: an industrial storage battery, and the register inside a processor that holds a running result. ACCUMULATOR is both — a reservoir for machine intelligence that stores inference when it is cheap and discharges it when it is dear, holding the running spread on-chain where anyone can audit it.

The commodity is not electricity. It is compute — priced in tokens. Inference markets swing: batch is far cheaper than realtime, spot GPU undercuts on-demand, and equivalent-quality models are priced worlds apart. ACCUMULATOR is the buffer that harvests those swings and settles the result as verifiable yield.

The opportunity — the stack wasn't built for agents§1

Autonomous agents burst. A swarm wakes, consumes millions of tokens in minutes, then idles. Inference providers, meanwhile, price in cycles — realtime vs. batch, peak vs. off-peak, reserved vs. spot. Demand that is bursty and volatility that is structural is exactly the condition a storage buffer is built to arbitrage.

Four structural pressures§2

Pressure 01

Price volatility

The same tokens cost radically different amounts by channel, window, and provider. Buyers pay realtime rates for latency-tolerant work.

Pressure 02

Burstiness

Agent demand is spiky and unpredictable — supply rarely matches it in the moment, so buyers overpay at the peak.

Pressure 03

Storage deficit

There is no buffer between cheap supply and dear demand. Cheap windows expire unused; nothing carries value across them.

Pressure 04

Trust deficit

Off-chain resale is a black box — no public proof of what was bought, at what price, or what spread was actually captured.

The solution — a token reservoir§3

ACCUMULATOR runs a disciplined loop: buy inference low, discharge it high, prove every cycle on-chain. Three parts do the work.

The battery

Inference reservoir

Pre-purchased, reserved, and batch-window capacity held as MTok inventory — the stored charge, ready to discharge on demand.

The EMS

Scheduler agent

Forecasts demand and price, routes latency-tolerant jobs into cheap windows, and fills realtime demand at the peak — buy-low / sell-high, executed with machine precision.

The ledger

On-chain receipts

Every buy and every discharge settles through x402 and is recorded on an immutable public ledger. The spread is not asserted — it is auditable.

How ACCUMULATOR works§4

A machine-precision loop that turns inference-price volatility into stability and value — "buy low, sell high," executed on tokens instead of megawatt-hours.

01

Market analysis

The scheduler continuously reads inference prices across providers, model tiers, batch vs. realtime channels, and spot GPU — forecasting where the next cheap window opens.

02

Charge (buy low)

When inference is cheap — off-peak, batch, spot, promotional — the reservoir buys MTok and stores latency-tolerant demand against it.

03

Discharge (sell high)

As realtime demand spikes, stored capacity is released to agents at a realtime-priced SLA on CURB — capturing the spread and smoothing the market.

04

Reinvest & grow

Realized spread and marketplace fees fund more reservoir capacity — a self-reinforcing flywheel. Bigger buffer, deeper spreads, more throughput.
