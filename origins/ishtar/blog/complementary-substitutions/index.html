Only a handful of underfunded forecasters, tobacco-fragrant Nick Land fanboys who can never stop collecting Burial reissues, avid LessWrong readers and Phil Tetlock disciples thought they knew what was going on—and a few hundred dozen Astral Codex Ten annual 75% discount Substack link hunters. Now, this is all markets, and he might not have been able to manage some margins. Risk happens. Unlike Michael Lewis tries to lobotomize the parts of your brains where you are supposed to understand behavioral markets, not everyone is as charismatic as Michael Burry because he is indeed Christian Bale.
