BOOSTER.

SOCIAL SIGNALS · TRENDS · FORECASTS — ANY FINANCIAL MARKET

Markets move on attention before they move on price. BOOSTER listens to the crowd — posts, threads, search bursts, the rotation of narratives — and compresses the noise into three instruments you can actually reason with.

№ 01 — SIGNALS

Crowd attention, measured.

Mention velocity, sentiment skew and narrative rotation across social feeds, compressed into one score per asset. When the crowd arrives early, the score says so — before the tape does.

№ 02 — TRENDS

Direction with persistence.

Which storylines are accelerating and which are exhausted. Trend flags separate a regime from a spike, so a loud afternoon never reads as a new market.

№ 03 — FORECASTS

Probabilities, not vibes.

Calibrated forecasts with explicit horizons and confidence bands — p10 / p50 / p90, scored in public against what actually happens. A forecast that can't be graded is an opinion.
