Marketplaces die.
Your work stays locked inside.

Foundation went offline on 27 April 2026 and told creators they would have to delist manually — then took the interface with them. Anything you listed is still sitting in the market contract. Zora's old auction house holds work the same way, and Drops contracts hold unwithdrawn sale proceeds nobody ever claimed. It is all still yours. There was just no longer a button.

What each action does§3

Cancel auction / buy price — returns the NFT to your wallet. Auctions can only be cancelled if nobody ever bid; one bid locks the piece until it settles.

Accept offer — this SELLS the piece for the WETH offered. Irreversible, and always behind a confirmation.

Withdraw — pulls sale proceeds sitting in a contract you own.

A piece with both an auction and a buy price needs needs both cancelled. The first transaction succeeds and looks green while the artwork stays in escrow — the contract holds it until the second lands. This app re-reads ownership after every action instead of trusting a green transaction.
