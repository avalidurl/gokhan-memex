Definition

The Tape Exchange is a fee desk and open launch tape: every coin launches with a fixed creator-fee tier, locked liquidity rules, and a public record of taxes, snipes, and survival — so fee income and bot behavior are readable, not rumored.

Fee desk
Launch with 1%, 2%, or 3% per trade. Most of it goes to the creator (wallet, X handle, holders, or buyback). The split is frozen at launch.
Open tape
Live board of launches, creator-tax cashflow, bot-filter heat, graduation/survival cohorts — Loophole-class analytics anyone can verify from chain logs.
$TAPEX
The platform token. The desk’s share of every trade is sold for quote; most of it buys and burns $TAPEX. Volume on the desk is the flywheel.
“Creators earn the rake. The tape stays public. The burn is the receipt.” — The Tape Exchange

Earn from trade one

Same Stonks-style loop: pick a fee tier, name where it pays, collect in the quote asset as soon as volume hits.

Platform cut → burn

~80% of the desk’s share buys $TAPEX on the market and burns it. Continuous buyback funded by every coin’s trades.

Product is the board

Multi-venue launch/tax tape, bot scores, fee distributions, alerts API — the general analytics product, not a single-chain meme.

Fee tiers

1% Steady

You ~0.70% · desk ~0.30%. Quiet volume, lighter friction.

2% Balanced

You ~1.50% · desk ~0.50%. Default for most launches.

3% Aggressive

You ~2.25% · desk ~0.75%. Max creator cash from day one.

Route your share to a wallet, an X account (claim by sign-in, no gas), holder rewards, or buyback-and-burn of your own coin. LP locked forever — only fees leave the position.
