Open Weights Falls

Even pizzerias are in the add-in markets and you wonder why you suck at your work of line. I think double-cheddar crusts with organic gluten free sesame are great and deserve any premia atop if the chef meets even the Barstool standards. While you are trying to time the markets reading into Pentagon pizza waves, though, you also tend to make sloppy generalizations regarding the open weight letters of global intents, lone ranger trail blazers diligently not bending before the “mob” so as not to turn a technology so future-present-impactful out of not only the empire business drives but also a firm belief in the supremacy of that which is to distill itself from not the future but immediate present. You have always done this. It is how you meet people online all the while thinking it’s not the people’s slop but is the AI slop.

That is, it is lazy to argue over no faith at all rather than having a good or bad faith, let alone the basics of business and economics, which is the pure human psyche. I think social media makes you dumb and you are responsible for weaving a proper network of critical thinking for your own. That was also why Dean Ball was unevenly, to put it roughly, “roasted” for a pristine communique regarding open source, open weights the last week, and it was done especially by people who should know how to speak better but anyway went to harvest populist slop online.

If you are wondering why you have both claude dot md and agents dot md in your file system today, and want to make sense of Huang’s first “tweet” or Sacks’ most of the time proper blanket criticism that at times won’t work, or the mere microeconomic basics such as substitute or complement, I think Gwern’s post from 2018 is a good primer: Laws of Tech: Commoditize Your Complement.
