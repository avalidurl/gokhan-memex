What I want more of is acceleration you can weigh: gigawatts, foundries, compute, mass to orbit. Hard tech, deep tech, manufacturing, spaceflight — capacity, not adjectives; the base the big problems don't get solved without. Governance should compete for that base like any other vendor: jurisdictions running different rules, rewarded on outcomes, not paperwork theater. My corner of it is the market plumbing — software that buys, sells, and settles with other software, fast enough to keep up with the factories we still have to build.
