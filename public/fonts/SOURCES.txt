Self-hosted OFL variable fonts for the memex's own pages (register,
journal, homepage). Restored original product designs keep their own faces
and do not load these files.

Source Sans 3
  Official files: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourcesans3
  Upstream: Adobe Source Sans 3 (https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans)
  License: OFL 1.1, Reserved Font Name "Source" (see source-sans-3/OFL.txt)

EB Garamond
  Official files: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ebgaramond
  Upstream: https://github.com/octaviopardo/EBGaramond12
  License: OFL 1.1 (see eb-garamond/OFL.txt)

Each face is a variable font with the weight axis restricted to 400–600,
then subset to latin and latin-ext unicode ranges (same ranges as the
existing JetBrains Mono files) and encoded as woff2 with fonttools
pyftsubset. To regenerate, from the official TTFs:

  fonttools varLib.instancer FONT.ttf wght=400:400:600
  pyftsubset INSTANCE.ttf --flavor=woff2 --unicodes=<range> --layout-features='*'
